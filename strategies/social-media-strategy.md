# Social Media Content Strategy — affiliates.malaclyde.com

## Target: Sleep Supplement (4GREATSLEEP)

### Platform Priority
1. **X/Twitter** — Best for text-based affiliate marketing, thread format works well
2. **YouTube Shorts** — Short-form video, affiliate link in description
3. **Reddit** — Community engagement (r/sleep, r/insomnia, r/supplements)

### Content Pillars

#### Pillar 1: Sleep Science Education (Build Trust)
- "Why do you wake up at 3am?" threads
- Sleep cycle explainers
- Common sleep myths debunked
- Link to product as "what I use"

#### Pillar 2: Personal Story / Social Proof
- "I tried everything for sleep. Here's what finally worked."
- Before/after narrative
- Relatable sleep struggles

#### Pillar 3: Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS)
- "Tossing and turning again?"
- "Your brain won't shut off at night?"
- Direct to solution

### UTM Strategy
```
https://affiliates.malaclyde.com/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=sleep-thread-01
```

### Posting Schedule
- 1 thread per day on X (educational or PAS)
- 1 Reddit comment per day in relevant subs (genuine engagement + subtle mention)
- Weekly YouTube Short script (submitted to worker-youtube-001)

- All affiliate links: `rel="sponsored nofollow"` (already in site code)

- No fake testimonials or misleading claims
